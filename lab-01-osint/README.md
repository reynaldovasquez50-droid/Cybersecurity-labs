# Lab 01 — OSINT & Secure Browsing

## Overview
Practiced open-source intelligence (OSINT) techniques and privacy-focused browsing configuration to reduce digital footprint and gather targeted information ethically.

## Tools Used
- Brave Browser
- DuckDuckGo
- Google Dorking
- Ground News
- Google Translate

## What I Did

### Privacy Configuration
- Configured Brave Browser with aggressive tracker/ad blocking and third-party cookie blocking
- Disabled Brave Rewards to minimize data exposure
- Switched default search engine to DuckDuckGo to avoid personalized tracking

### Google Dorking
Used advanced search operators to locate publicly available cybersecurity policy documents:
filetype:pdf "password policy" site:edu

- filetype:pdf — restricts results to PDF documents only
- "password policy" — exact phrase match
- site:edu — limits results to educational institution domains

### Media Bias Analysis
- Used Ground News to analyze coverage across 86 sources
- Identified bias distribution: 42% Center, 41% Left, 17% Right
- Compared AI summary vs Ground News multi-source breakdown

### AI Translation Validation
- Tested reverse translation: English to Spanish back to English
- Confirmed meaning was preserved, validating AI translation reliability

## Key Takeaways
- Google Dorking surfaces files not found by standard searches
- Always cross-reference AI-generated content against multiple sources
- Privacy browsers reduce tracking but don't eliminate it entirely=
